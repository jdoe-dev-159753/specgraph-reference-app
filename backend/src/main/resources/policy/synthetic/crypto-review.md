# Synthetic crypto review guidance

This demonstration policy is synthetic and exists only to exercise grounded retrieval. It is not institutional policy and does not establish a production crypto-risk rule.

Crypto activity may merit additional human review when transaction value, novelty and destination context combine, for example a newly observed destination, a sudden increase in crypto transfer activity, a new exchange relationship, or several transfers that differ materially from the customer's recent pattern. Blockchain or exchange identity alone is not proof of suspicious behavior.

Reviewers should inspect the persisted transaction evidence first. Detector outputs remain derived signals, retrieved policy remains contextual evidence, and generated model output remains advisory synthesis. The history record should preserve the identity and retrieval score of the policy chunk used by the analysis.
