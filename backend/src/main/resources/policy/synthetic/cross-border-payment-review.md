# Synthetic cross-border payment review guidance

This demonstration policy is synthetic and exists only to exercise grounded retrieval. It is not institutional policy and must not be presented as a production compliance rule.

Cross-border payment activity may warrant additional human review when value, frequency, destination pattern and change from prior behavior combine meaningfully. Useful context includes a rapid increase in transfer volume, repeated high-value payments, newly observed receiver-bank countries, or several new counterparties in a short period. Country information alone is not a decision rule.

The review record should distinguish persisted payment facts from retrieved policy context, derived detector evidence and generated analysis. Any recommendation should cite the relevant retrieved policy chunk and retain its retrieval provenance so that a reviewer can reconstruct why the context was supplied.
