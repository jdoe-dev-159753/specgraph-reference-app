# Synthetic card review guidance

This demonstration policy is synthetic and exists only to exercise grounded retrieval. It is not institutional policy and does not define a production fraud or AML decision rule.

Card activity merits additional human review when several contextual indicators align, for example unusually high-value card-not-present activity, repeated declines followed by approval, rapid repeated attempts, or a sudden change from the customer's recent activity pattern. A single card-not-present transaction is not by itself proof of suspicious behavior.

Review should preserve the source transaction facts separately from any detector score or generated explanation. The reviewer should inspect the original card activity, source-shaped risk assessments, related recent activity, and the provenance of any derived detector signal before deciding whether escalation is warranted.
